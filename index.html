The problem

When warning arrives too late

The sequence

Sense. Analyze. Detect. Communicate. Warn. Record.

01

Sense

Sensors continuously observe temperature, humidity, smoke and vibration in the environment.

02

Analyze

Local processing on the node examines current conditions against expected ranges.

03

Detect

Abnormal or hazardous patterns are identified without waiting on a remote server.

04

Communicate

Critical information travels over LoRa — a long-range, low-power wireless link.

05

Warn

The gateway delivers visual, audible and voice warnings at the point of need.

06

Record

Events are timestamped and logged locally for later review.

Offline-first architecture

The internet is a bonus — not a requirement

The core warning path is designed to remain functional without depending on continuous internet connectivity.
